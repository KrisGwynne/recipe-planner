Recipe planner · PP-145

Foundations

Colours, type and the basic pieces every screen is built from. Phone-first: component demos are shown at 390px, the width of a typical phone.

Principles

  • One hand, one thumb

    Main actions sit in the bottom half of the screen. Every tap target is at least 44px.

  • Readable at arm's length

    17px body text and large numbers for quantities and timers, for reading from the worktop.

  • No photos

    The app stores no images, so cards rely on type, badges and numbers rather than pictures.

  • Estimates look different

    Estimated nutrition always has a dashed outline and a ~ prefix, never colour alone.

Colour

Warm paper background with a tomato accent. Meat, fish, veg and vegan each get a colour, used only for those badges and filters.

Bottom navigation

Five tabs: Recipes, Plans, Import (raised, as the main way new things come in), Compare and Household.

On the web: the bar is fixed to the bottom and padded for the iPhone home indicator; it stays visible as Safari's toolbar shrinks. From tablet width up it becomes a side rail, and the Import button moves into it.

Alternative if Compare turns out to be rarely used: move it into the recipe list (select recipes → Compare) and give the tab slot to a shopping list later.

Decisions

Approved by Kris on 11 October 2026.

  • Tomato accent, warm background, serif recipe names: kept.
  • Dark mode follows the phone's setting.
  • Five bottom tabs as shown, with web-specific handling (safe area, side rail on wider screens).
  • Printed vs estimated: dashed outline and ~ prefix.
  • New: a Vegan category alongside Meat / Fish / Veg, taken from the recipe's dietary flags.

Compare recipes

The Compare tab: pick 2 to 4 recipes and see their calories and macros per serving side by side, where those calories come from, and a table of everything else. Printed and estimated nutrition always look different. Built in PP-173; comparing two meal plans (PP-174) is left for later.

Questions for review

  1. One chart per nutrient (C, D) rather than a single grouped bar chart: kcal is in the hundreds and grams in the tens, so on one axis the macro bars would be slivers. Recommend: as shown.
  2. Which nutrients get charts (C, D): kcal, protein, carbs and fat, with sugars, saturates, fibre and salt in the table only. Recommend: as shown; salt might earn a chart later.
  3. Coming back to the tab (A, C): should Compare remember the last comparison on this device, so leaving and coming back keeps it? The comparison is in the page address either way, so Back and shared links work. Recommend: remember it.
  4. No written differences (C): with two recipes, the page could say "153 kcal fewer, 2g less protein". Recommend: leave it out for now; the bars and numbers already show it.
  5. Library shortcut (B): the design brings recipes in only through the picker and a recipe page. Should the library also get a "Select to compare" mode? Recommend: not now; the recipe page's "Compare with…" covers the common case.

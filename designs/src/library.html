Recipe library

Browsing, filtering, sorting and searching a household's recipes. Built from the approved foundations. Example library: 24 recipes; the two real examples plus made-up ones.

Browsing

Filtering

Within a section, ticking more options widens the results (Pasta or Rice). Across sections they narrow (Pasta and Fish). Each option shows how many recipes it would find with everything else you've picked; options that would find nothing are greyed out but still tappable.

Sorting and empty states

Wider screens

Desktop is a nice-to-have, so this is the same pieces rearranged rather than a separate design: the bottom bar becomes a side rail, filters sit in a panel on the left instead of a sheet, and cards flow into columns.

Decisions

Approved by Kris on 11 October 2026.

  • Default sort is Name, A to Z.
  • "Cook with" shows recipes that need nothing beyond the ticked equipment.
  • Cards keep time, kcal and protein. Filter counts stay.
  • New: a Source filter (book, magazine or website by name; family recipes grouped), and search also matches source names.

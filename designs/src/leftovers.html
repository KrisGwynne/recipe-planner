Recipe planner · PP-180

Leftovers and the freezer

What happens to the servings a meal doesn't use: eaten later in the same plan, eaten in the next plan, or frozen. The freezer keeps a list of what's in it, so meals can be planned from it. Builds on the meal plans design (screens there are named "meal plans D" and so on). The example is the same plan, "This week", Mon 12 – Sun 18 Oct, for 2 people; today is Mon 12.

How it works

A planned recipe is either cooked (as today) or leftovers, eaten from an earlier cook or from the freezer. Leftovers count toward each person's nutrition like any meal, but aren't cooked again (and won't add to a shopping list, PP-187).

A cook makes the recipe's servings. What the people at that meal don't eat is left over, and each serving left over can be planned for a later meal (this plan or the next) or frozen. Whatever isn't planned or frozen still shows the amber "N left over", so nothing is forgotten.

The freezer is the household's list of frozen servings: recipe, servings and when they went in. Servings get there from a plan ("freeze them") or by hand (batch cooking), and leave when a plan eats them or someone takes them out.

Leftovers in a plan

The freezer

The freezer lives on the Plans tab, since that's where it's used. It's the household's: anyone in it sees and changes the same list.

Other details settled here

  • A leftovers meal is for everyone in the plan, like any meal: people × portions each. Half portions cover one serving shared or a light lunch; a meal for just one person isn't covered here.
  • Leftovers can come from this plan or the plan just before it (the latest plan that started earlier). Nothing older: anything that old should be in the freezer.
  • Copy to a new plan (meal plans J) copies leftovers meals along with their cooks in the same plan. Leftovers from the plan before and meals from the freezer aren't copied, and the toast says how many were left out.
  • Nutrition (meal plans H) counts leftovers meals like any other, including ones from the freezer or last week. The plan list's "N meals" counts them too.
  • "Cooked today" on the recipe page isn't touched by any of this; the cooking history stays what people mark.

Decisions

Approved by Kris on 18 October 2026, going with every recommendation.

  • The freezer is a row on the Plans tab that opens its own page (G, H), not a sixth tab.
  • The freezer keeps itself up to date: servings go in on the cook's day and come out once the planned meal's day has passed, with no ticking off. When real life differs, fix the count (I).
  • Fridge leftovers that will be 2 or more days old by the meal get an amber warning in the picker (D), but can still be planned.
  • Leftovers can come from this plan or the plan just before it, nothing older.
  • Adding to the freezer by hand (J) is in, from the freezer page and the recipe page's ⋯ menu.
  • Split in two: PP-180 builds leftovers in a plan (A to E and L, without the freezer parts); PP-189 builds the freezer (F to K, and the freezer parts of B, D, E and L), after it.

Recipe planner · PP-152

Household settings and account

The Household tab: the household's name, its members and roles, invite links, downloading every recipe, signing out, and the steps for leaving, removing someone, deleting the household and deleting your account. Owners see everything; members see the household and their own account.

The Household tab

One scrolling page in four groups: the household and its people, recipes, your account, then leaving and deleting at the bottom, away from everyday taps.

Name, invites and members (owners)

Leaving and deleting

There's always at least one owner. The steps change with who you are: a member, one of several owners, the only owner with others in the household, or the only person in it.

Deleting your account without a household

Added for PP-183. Someone who isn't in a household (they left, were removed, saw it deleted, or never made one) only sees "Start a household", so the Household tab's "Delete my account…" can't be reached. These two screens give them a way.

Wider screens

From tablet width the page splits in two: the household and its people on the left, recipes, account and leave or delete on the right. Sheets open as dialogs.

Decisions

Approved by Kris on 14 October 2026.

  • A household can have several owners. "Make an owner" and "Make a member" replace a separate Transfer ownership step; handing over is making someone an owner, then leaving.
  • Only owners invite people, as the handover says.
  • Typing the household's name is asked for only when the household and its recipes would be deleted (J, L). Everything else uses a plain confirmation.
  • Everyone in the household sees each other's Google email in the member list.
  • Invite links can only be copied when they're made. The open links list offers Cancel only.

N and O added for PP-183 on 17 October 2026. Kris asked Claude to go with its recommendations while he was away, so these are open to change when he reviews them.

  • "Delete my account…" sits beside Sign out on "Start a household", in the same grey, not red (N).
  • It's shown to everyone on that page, including first-time users.
  • The confirmation is K's plain sheet, without the household wording (O).

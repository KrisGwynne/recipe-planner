Recipe planner · PP-150

Meal plans

Making a plan, filling its days and meals with recipes, setting portions per person, and seeing what each person eats a day and where there'll be leftovers. The example plan is for 2 people, Monday 12 to Sunday 18 October, mostly dinners, as a real week would start.

Plans

The Plans tab lists the household's plans by date. A plan is a name, a start date, a number of days (7 unless changed) and how many people it feeds (2 unless changed).

Filling a plan

On a phone a 7 × 4 grid doesn't fit, so the plan is a list of days, each holding its meals in order: breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack. Empty meals are small "+" buttons at the bottom of each day, so a mostly-dinners plan stays short.

Totals and leftovers

Everything is per person: a recipe's nutrition per serving × that person's portions, added up per day. The plan average is over the days that have something planned, and says how many.

Changing and deleting plans

Wider screens

From tablet width the plan becomes the grid the phone can't fit: days across, meals down, and each day's total along the bottom. Tapping a cell or a recipe opens the same sheets, as dialogs.

An empty cell turns tomato on hover (Tue lunch here). A 14-day plan scrolls sideways a week at a time. Below tablet width the phone layout (D) is used.

Decisions

Approved by Kris on 13 October 2026.

  • On a phone the plan is a list of day cards with a sticky day strip; empty meals are "+" buttons. From tablet width it's the full grid.
  • The plan average is over days with at least one meal planned, and says how many.
  • Leftovers are per recipe, as in the spec. Planning a later meal as "leftovers from Monday" is an optional future ticket (PP-180).
  • New: "Copy to a new plan" in the plan's ⋯ menu, built with PP-170.
  • The recipe picker starts filtered to the meal being added (removable).

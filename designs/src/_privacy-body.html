Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Recipe planner is a personal project run by Kris Gwynne in the UK. This page says what it stores about you, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions or requests: contact email.

What's stored

  • From Google, when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo, and the ID Google gives your account. The app never sees your Google password.
  • Your household: its name, who's in it and whether they're an owner, and invite links (stored only in a scrambled form, and they stop working after 7 days).
  • What you add: the recipes you import, and your household's favourites, ratings, notes, cooking history and meal plans.
  • To keep it working: a sign-in cookie (it lasts 30 days), how many recipes you've imported in the last hour (to stop the app being flooded), and the name of the household you last left, to tell you you're no longer in it.
  • On your device: a cookie remembering whether you like the library as cards or a list, and your last comparison, which stays in your browser.

Who can see it

The people in your household see its recipes, plans and notes, and each other's names, email addresses and photos. Nobody outside your household does. Nothing is sold or shared, and there are no adverts, analytics or trackers.

Where it's kept

The app runs on Vercel and its database is with Neon, in region. Google handles signing in. Each of them only processes your data to run the app.

How long it's kept

Until you delete it. Sign-ins end after 30 days without use, and the import count after an hour. Deleted data can stay in the database's backups for up to 7 days.

Deleting it

All of this is on the Household tab, and works straight away:

  • Download all recipes gives you a copy of every recipe file first, if you want one.
  • Delete my account deletes your Google details, sign-ins and membership. Recipes, notes and plans you added to a shared household stay with it, without your name.
  • Delete household (owners) deletes its recipes, plans, notes and invite links for everyone.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing contact email. If you're unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will be shown in the app first.
